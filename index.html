Beyond the hardware

A mission can end. Its contribution can continue.

“To understand what remains, we must first remember what each explorer made possible.”

The equipment left on other worlds is more than a collection of machines. It represents experiments attempted, landscapes examined, engineering challenges overcome, and knowledge passed on to future missions.
